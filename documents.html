Preparation

Know what you'll need, before you need it.

A general overview of documents most students are asked to prepare during the application process.

Start gathering these early.

Documents can take time to locate, translate or attest — starting early keeps your application on schedule. Once you have these ready, our team helps you prepare everything else your chosen universities ask for.

Documents You May Need

  • Class 12 Mark Sheet / Certificate
  • Bachelor's Degree Certificate
  • Bachelor's Degree Mark Sheets / Official Transcripts
  • Valid Passport
  • Language Proficiency Certificate
