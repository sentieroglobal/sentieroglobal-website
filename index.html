FAQs

Questions, answered.

Why study in Italy?

Italy offers globally recognised universities, diverse programs and a rich cultural and academic environment within Europe.

Who can apply through SentieroGlobal?

We work with 12th pass students, graduates, working professionals and families exploring international education options.

Do you help with Bachelor's applications?

Yes, we support undergraduate applicants through profile assessment, university selection and the application process.

Do you help with Master's applications?

Yes, we support postgraduate applicants across a range of academic fields and destinations.

Can you help me choose a university?

We help match your academic profile, goals and preferences with suitable university and course options.

Do you help with SOP, CV and LOR?

Yes, we provide guidance for preparing CVs, statements of purpose and recommendation letters.

Do you provide scholarship guidance?

We help students understand relevant scholarship and funding opportunities they may be eligible for.

Do you help with CIMEA / DoV?

Yes, we offer guidance and assistance related to CIMEA / Declaration of Value requirements. Additional charges may apply.

Do you help with visa preparation?

We provide step-by-step guidance for student visa documentation and submission.

What documents are needed for an Italy student visa?

Typical documents include a passport, visa application form, proof of admission, academic documents and proof of financial support, among others. Requirements may vary — see our Visa Guidance section for details.

Do you provide post-arrival support?

Yes, subject to location and individual requirements, including guidance on local registration and ongoing support.

Can you help with countries other than Italy?

While Italy is our primary focus, we also guide students interested in Germany, France, Spain, Netherlands, Ireland, Poland and other destinations.

How can I book a consultation?

Use the "Book a Consultation" button anywhere on this site, or reach out directly via call or WhatsApp.
