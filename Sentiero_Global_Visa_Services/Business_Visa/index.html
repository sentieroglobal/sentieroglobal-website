How Sentiero Global Assists

Country-specific visa requirements change frequently and are set by the relevant embassy or authority — so our role is to help you prepare thoroughly, not to promise a particular result.

Initial Consultation

We understand your situation, destination and timeline before recommending next steps.

Documentation Guidance

A clear list of the documents your application is likely to need.

Form-Filling Assistance

Support completing application forms accurately and consistently.

Supporting Document Review

A review pass on your documents before they're submitted.

Application Organisation

Everything organised and sequenced the way the relevant authority expects.

Interview Preparation

Where an interview applies, practical preparation for what to expect.

Sentiero Global provides guidance, documentation preparation and application support only. The final decision on any visa application always belongs to the relevant embassy, consulate or immigration authority — we do not offer, and no one can offer, a guaranteed outcome.
